Who We Are

Two families. One mission.

Shared Grounds and Sons was founded by two families in Tampa Bay — each raising a son with Down syndrome, one also on the autism spectrum. We started this business to build something bigger than coffee: real jobs, real skills, and a real future for people who are too often overlooked by the workforce.

Every bag is packed by hand by people with special needs, right here in the community — not outsourced to a warehouse.

Our Story

From banana bread to barrels.

We started in January 2025 as Three to One in the Kitchen, a small banana bread operation. In August 2025 we pivoted to coffee — and it changed everything.

Every bag is sourced from Hog Batch, a specialty roaster in St. Petersburg, FL, then aged in bourbon, rum, or whiskey barrels for a flavor you won't find on a grocery shelf.

In April 2026, we rebranded to Shared Grounds and Sons — a name that reflects who we are: two families sharing this work with our sons, and with our community of packers.
